## 2. Decision: what to sell

### ✅ Primary offer: "Bhandup Local Growth Kit"
**Get found on Google, look good on Instagram, and turn enquiries into bookings for local clinics, gyms, salons and coaching classes.**

Why this one:
- **Fits your skills.** You already produce video (PS Unfiltered / Unseen Depth of Life), and reels are the hardest part for shop owners.
- **₹0 to start.** Your phone, CapCut/VN, Canva free, the free WhatsApp Business app and free Google Business Profile.
- **Fits 5–10 hrs/week.** Batch-shoot once every two weeks per client, and AI tools do the heavy editing and captions.
- **The pain is visible.** Owners can see their own rating, review count and dead Instagram. Selling it takes about 2 minutes.
- **Recurring revenue.** A monthly retainer instead of one-off gigs.

| Package | What they get | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter: Google Fix** (one-time) | Claim/verify GBP, 10+ photos, services and prices, correct hours and category, a QR "review us" standee, a WhatsApp Business catalogue and auto-replies | **₹2,999–4,999** |
| **Growth** (monthly) | 8 reels/month (2 shoots), 4 GBP posts, review-reply handling, monthly report (calls, direction requests, reviews) | **₹7,999/month** |
| **Growth+** (monthly) | Growth plus 12 reels, a festive campaign (Navratri, Diwali, New Year, Gudi Padwa), and WhatsApp broadcast templates for offers | **₹11,999/month** |

**Target clients, in order:** (1) new dental, skin and physio clinics, (2) gyms and fitness studios, (3) salons and spas, (4) coaching and tuition classes, (5) restaurants and cafés.

### 🔜 Upsell from month 3: AI WhatsApp receptionist
Auto-reply, appointment booking and reminders on the WhatsApp Business API (platforms like Interakt or AiSensy cost the client about ₹1.5k–2.5k/month plus Meta message fees). You charge **₹5k setup plus ₹2k/month** for managing it. Sell this only to existing clients who trust you.

### ❌ Rejected, and why
- **Building websites:** a crowded market with low prices (Fiverr/Instagram freelancers), and local shops care more about Maps and Instagram.
- **Physical product / food:** needs more than ₹10k, an FSSAI licence and a lot of time.
- **Cold email to local shops:** they live on WhatsApp and walk-ins, not email. Client Scout fits better later for B2B or online clients.

## 7. Risks and mitigations
- **Clients delay payment:** take the first month in advance by UPI. Pilots are capped at 2.
- **Time overrun with a 5–10 hr cap:** cap reels per client, batch-shoot, and use templates.
- **Fake-review temptation:** never buy reviews (Google penalises the profile). Only the QR asks real customers.
- **Medical advertising rules:** clinic reels must avoid "guaranteed cure" or before/after claims that break the NMC/Dental Council ethics codes. Stick to education and clinic-tour content.
