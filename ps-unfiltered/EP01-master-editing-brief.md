# PS UNFILTERED — MASTER VIDEO EDITING BRIEF (EP01: Discipline Over Motivation)
Tagline: Think Deeper. Live Better. Build Wisely.
(Supersedes section 5 "Editing brief" and the black/gold visual notes in the publish kit.)

1. Direction: Raj Shamani hooks/pacing · Prakhar Gupta depth/pauses · Ankur Warikoo structure/clean visuals · Varun Mayya subtle purposeful edits. Own PS Unfiltered identity, no imitation.
2. Clean-up first: remove stumbles, repeats, lost thoughts, false starts, fillers, long pauses, extra breaths. Keep emotional pauses. Seamless joins. Keep meaning & order. No invented dialogue / AI speech. Unrepairable sentence → remove whole.
3. Opening (0–15s): strongest real line first, no logo/greeting, subtle punch-in, minimal text, brand identity after hook.
4. Look: white/light-grey bg, natural skin, neutral whites, subtle warm highlights; fix exposure/WB/contrast/sharpness; consistent grade; subtle depth; no bg replacement or face edits; black/charcoal clothing contrast.
5. Camera/pacing: subtle punch-ins, alternate medium/close, cut dead space, no excessive zooms/shakes/transitions, let key lines breathe.
6. B-roll only when it strengthens the point (motivation start, routines, fitness, saving/investing, phone/Netflix distraction, journaling/self-trust, future self). Restrained motion graphics. No random stock.
7. Captions: accurate, synced, clean modern type, only key words in muted amber/gold, safe margins, no excessive word-by-word animation, short emphasis text for big lines.
8. Audio: subtle cinematic music, low, dipped/removed on emotional lines; dialogue NR + EQ, compression, loudness normalisation; no unnecessary SFX/risers.
9. Branding: minimal title treatment after hook, tagline, clean outro + natural CTA, never interrupting.
10. QC: full watch, cut check, no clipped words, subtitle accuracy, consistent colour/audio, narrative coherent.
11. Deliverables: (1) 16:9 full video, 4K if source supports; (2) clean dialogue mix; (3) .srt; (4) three 9:16 Shorts; (5) one thumbnail concept.
Rule: never fabricate footage or dialogue. Every edit must improve clarity, retention, emotion or understanding.
